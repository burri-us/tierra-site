About Tierra LLC

Mission

Help organizations and individuals build and run what matters with software they can trust and evidence they can show.

How we work

  • People decide: our tools prepare and record; named people approve.

  • Privacy first: our websites use no cookies or tracking, and AssuredForge's desktop edition stores your records on your own PCour products keep your records under your control.

  • Plain and practical: we build what helps you do the work, and leave out what doesn't.

  • Evidence: every governed decision is recorded and verifiable.

Founder

Tierra LLC was founded by Ram Burri to build software that makes accountability practical rather than burdensome. AssuredForge is our first product.

AssuredForge is developed and operated by Tierra LLC, a Michigan-based software company focused on software delivery governance, traceability and release records you can export.
